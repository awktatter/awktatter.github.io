A collection of small thoughts and insights that once roamed the twisty nerve cauldrons of my brain 

---

You will be able to relax in spaces that you don't have stay vigilant, and maybe even feel a sense of safety. The space can be as simple as your home, or a place far-away, but also just your body.

---

Humans cannot do complete detachment. We are bound to be attached to something or someone in our lives. And it's not wrong. What matters more is how we handle the attachment. Do we let it dictate our choices or reactions? Or do we recognize that only parts are within our control and focus on them

---

It's gonna take a while to make this stick - "Responses are not immediate".

---

Postivity doesn't have to be forceful or hard. Start out by focusing on the good things that happened (either because of you or something else). Even on a bad day

---

Humans are pattern-searchers and meaning-makers. It's what you'll end up doing over a period of time. Don't do that in every instant. There isn't much and you're not going to find any.
